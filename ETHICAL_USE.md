# Ethical use

IntervAIew is for deliberate interview practice only.

Do not use it for unauthorized live-interview assistance, deception, hidden realtime answers, recruitment-test automation, monitoring/screen-sharing evasion, anti-detection, system-audio capture, or recording without informed consent. Users are responsible for following employer rules, platform terms, privacy law, and all applicable policies.

The project intentionally omits hidden windows, capture avoidance, live answer generation, scoring claims, and real-interview integrations.
