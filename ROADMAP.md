# Roadmap

## MVP (current)

Deterministic Mock and real text-provider adapters; personalized question plans; transactional text interview; transcript, history, export, and deletion.

## Phase two: consent-based voice practice

```text
DeepSeek Question Planning
          ↓
OpenAI Realtime Interviewer
          ↓
Transcript Synchronizer
          ↓
Web Audio + MediaRecorder
```

Priorities: implement a separate OpenAI Realtime adapter, explicit media consent/permission lifecycle, and synchronized interviewer/candidate recording with local retention controls. Text and realtime providers remain independent.

## Later candidates

Accessibility audits, database backup/import, configurable retention, provider observability without content logging, and additional deterministic practice formats. Scoring, covert assistance, and multi-agent orchestration are not planned without a separate ethical/product review.
